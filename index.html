Ben & Friends

A Passion That Became a Calling

Hey there! I'm Ben, and I've been crazy about dogs since before I could walk. What started as helping neighbors watch their pups turned into Benny and the Pets — a boarding facility where dogs don't just stay, they thrive.

Every dog that walks through our doors becomes part of our pack. We believe in spacious play areas, personalized attention, and yes — unlimited belly rubs.
