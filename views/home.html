Before you book

How booking works

  1. 1. Create an account

    Add your dog's details and upload their current vaccination record. Documents are stored privately and only staff who admit your dog can see them.

  2. 2. Send a request

    Choose a service and your dates. A request is not a confirmed booking — we check availability first.

  3. 3. We approve it

    {{approval_timeline}} You'll get an email either way.

  4. 4. Pay securely

    Once approved you get a secure payment link. Card details are entered on our payment provider's own page and never reach our servers. Payment and deposit terms.

  5. 5. Drop-off day

    Bring food, any medication, and anything that helps your dog settle. Read the boarding agreement and the document policy before your first stay.
